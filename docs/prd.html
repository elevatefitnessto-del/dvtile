Summary

dvtile.ca is the main sales tool for DV Tile (Don Valley Tile), a Toronto flooring contractor that installs marble, granite, natural stone, large-format porcelain and tile. The site exists to turn affluent GTA homeowners, and the designers and builders who work for them, into booked site visits.

Version 1 replaces a showroom-style page with a contractor site built around craftsmanship. It opens on a limestone manor at blue hour, walks the visitor through eight close-up installation details, and presents the full Olympia Tile + Stone catalogue as something DV Tile supplies and installs.

One job: a homeowner in Forest Hill, Rosedale or Oakville finishes the page believing DV Tile can be trusted with Calacatta in their ensuite, and books a site visit.

Audience

Primary: affluent GTA homeowners

  • Owners of detached homes in Forest Hill, Rosedale, Lawrence Park, the Bridle Path, Hoggs Hollow, the Kingsway, Kleinburg, King City and Oakville.
  • Mid-renovation or planning one: ensuites, kitchens, whole-floor stone, terraces and pools. Often a new build or a gut renovation.
  • They buy on trust and evidence of care, not price. Their fear is a visible mistake in an expensive material: lippage, mismatched veins, a leaking shower.
  • They browse on a phone in the evening and come back on a laptop to decide.

Secondary: the trade

  • Interior designers and architects who need an installer who follows drawings and protects their reputation.
  • Custom home builders who need a reliable stone sub-trade with capacity.

Positioning

Statement: For GTA homeowners finishing a fine home in stone, DV Tile is the installation crew that treats a floor like joinery: laid out on paper first, prepared properly, and set flat to the millimetre, with the full Olympia range to choose from.

Proof points the site must carry

  • A layout drawing and dry-laid slabs approved before any cut.
  • Lippage under a millimetre on large format, through levelling and full mortar coverage.
  • Showers waterproofed and flood-tested before tile.
  • One crew from preparation to sealing.
  • Official installation partner of Olympia Tile + Stone, plus Centura.

Voice

Calm, specific, tradesman-precise. Talk in materials, millimetres and steps, never in superlatives. No invented statistics, testimonials or awards: add them only when they are real.

Brand identity

Logo

Four square tiles in a 2 × 2 grid with a bronze marble vein running through them in an S-curve, cut cleanly at each grout joint. The vein stands for the Don Valley and the parkway that follows it; matching it across four tiles shows the book-matching skill DV Tile sells. Wordmark: DV TILE in wide-tracked Familjen Grotesk, with Don Valley Tile in Pinyon Script beneath.

  • Light backgrounds: green tiles, bronze vein. Dark backgrounds: pale marble tiles, bronze vein.
  • Files: brand/mark.svg, brand/mark-reverse.svg, and horizontal and stacked PNG lockups in brand/.
  • Clear space: the width of one tile on every side. Minimum mark size: 24 px.

Experience and quality

  • Works from 360 px phones to 2560 px displays with no sideways scrolling.
  • WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, visible focus rings, 44 px touch targets, labelled form fields, skip link.
  • Reduced motion: the walkthrough jumps between details and the hero shows without its entrance.
  • Without JavaScript, every caption and section is still readable in order.
  • No third-party trackers or chat widgets without an explicit decision.

Phase 2: real work

  • Photograph every finished job: one wide, three details, one in raking light that proves flatness.
  • Replace concept frames with a real walkthrough once three strong projects exist.
  • Collect written client reviews with permission to publish name and neighbourhood.

Olympia content rules

Use Olympia’s category names, product names and logo as their partner. Link to their pages for specifications and photos rather than copying descriptions or images, unless Olympia confirms in writing that dealers may reuse them.

Local SEO

  • Title and description lead with "marble, stone and tile flooring contractor in Toronto".
  • Structured data: HomeAndConstructionBusiness with areaServed and services; FAQPage for the FAQ.
  • Claim and complete the Google Business Profile as DV Tile (Don Valley Tile), with the same name, address and phone as the site.
  • Phase 2: one page per high-value service (marble floors, shower tile, kitchen backsplash, heated floors) and per cluster of neighbourhoods, each with real project photos.
  • Earn links from Olympia’s installer listings, designer partners and local renovation guides.

Technical

  • Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step and no libraries, hosted on Vercel from the elevatefitnessto-del/dvtile repository; pushes to main deploy production.
  • Form: Vercel serverless function sending through Resend. Needs RESEND_API_KEY, APPOINTMENTS_TO and APPOINTMENTS_FROM set in the project.
  • Walkthrough: 281 WebP frames per orientation (about 24 MB landscape, 11 MB portrait), loaded coarse-first only when the walkthrough is near, cached as immutable.
  • Fonts from Google Fonts. Security headers for nosniff, referrer and permissions policy.

Risks and open questions

  • Rendered imagery: buyers may assume the concept home is a real project. Mitigation: footer disclosure now, real photography in phase 2.
  • Address: is 44 Jackes Avenue a place clients should visit, or a mailing address only?
  • Phone: no number is published yet, and luxury buyers often call before they fill a form.
  • Form delivery: confirm the three Resend variables are set in Vercel, then send a test request.
  • Olympia assets: confirm whether Olympia allows partners to reuse product photography.
  • Centura: no official Centura logo is in the repository; add one if they provide it.
  • Page weight: walkthrough frames are heavy on cellular data. Watch real-user metrics and lower frame quality if needed.
