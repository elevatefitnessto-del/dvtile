Stone restoration and polishing

Honing, polishing, crack and chip repair, regrouting and sealing for marble and stone floors that have lost their finish.

Book a site assessment

What we restore

  • Dull, etched or scratched marble and limestone
  • Uneven tiles where edges have worn proud
  • Cracked or chipped stone, filled and colour-matched
  • Stained or failing grout, cleaned or replaced
  • Stone that needs resealing after cleaning

How restoration works

  1. We assess the stone, finish and damage on site and test a small area so you can see the result before we start.
  2. Floors are ground flat where needed, honed through progressively finer grits, then polished or left honed to suit the room.
  3. Repairs are filled with colour-matched resin and blended into the surrounding stone.
  4. The floor is sealed and you receive care instructions to keep the finish longer.

Questions

Can etching from acids be removed?

Usually. Light etching can be honed and polished out. Deep damage may need grinding, which we assess on site.

Can you match a replacement tile?

We try to match from the same stone type and finish. Natural stone varies, so we show you the replacement before setting it.
