Why large format needs a different crew

  1. Big panels magnify any unevenness in the floor or wall, so we flatten the substrate first rather than relying on mortar to hide it.
  2. Each panel is back-buttered and set with a levelling system for full coverage and even edges.
  3. Slabs are moved and set with suction lifters and slab carts, so corners and edges arrive undamaged.
  4. We plan cuts and seams on a drawing so joints line up with cabinetry, windows and fixtures.

Questions

What is lippage and why does it matter?

Lippage is the height difference between neighbouring tiles. With large panels and raking light from big windows, even a small step catches the eye. We aim for under a millimetre.

Can porcelain slabs go in a shower?

Yes. Slabs make a shower wall with very few joints. They go over a waterproofing membrane that is flood-tested before tile.
