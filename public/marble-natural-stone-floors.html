  • Thresholds, stair treads and hearths cut from the same stone

How we lay stone

  1. We check the subfloor for flatness, deflection and moisture before quoting, because natural stone shows every flaw beneath it.
  2. You approve a layout drawing showing where joints, cuts and veins fall. For slab and feature floors we dry-lay pieces so you can see the veining before anything is set.
  3. Stone goes down over an uncoupling membrane where the substrate calls for it, with full mortar coverage so the floor sounds solid and edges sit flat.
  4. Joints are grouted and the stone sealed for the finish and room, and you receive care instructions for that material.

Questions

Can marble go in a kitchen or entry?

Yes, with the right finish. Honed marble hides wear and etching better than polished; we talk through finish and sealing for each room at the site assessment.

Do you install marble over in-floor heating?

Yes. Heating mats go in first, are tested with the thermostat, and the stone is set over an uncoupling layer so the heat cycle does not crack joints.
