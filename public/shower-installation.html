Showers and wet rooms

Curbless entries, linear drains, custom niches, benches and steam showers, waterproofed and flood-tested before a single tile goes on.

Book a site assessment

What we build

  • Curbless and walk-in showers with linear drains
  • Custom recessed niches, mitred at the corners and lit with LED where you want it
  • Built-in benches and ledges in tile, slab or solid stone
  • Steam showers with sloped ceilings and vapour-tight assemblies
  • Shower jambs and thresholds in solid stone

Waterproofing first

  1. We build the shower pan to slope correctly to the drain and install a bonded waterproofing membrane over the walls and floor.
  2. The pan is flood-tested and left to stand before any tile is installed, so a problem shows up while it is cheap to fix.
  3. Niches and benches are framed and waterproofed as part of the same system, not added afterwards.
  4. We coordinate with your plumber on valve, rainhead and drain positions so tile layout and fixtures line up.

Questions

Do you install the plumbing fixtures?

We install the tile, stone and waterproofing and work alongside your plumber, who installs valves, rainheads and drains.

Can you add LED lighting to a niche?

Yes. We plan the LED channel when the niche is framed so the light is hidden and the strip sits behind waterproofing. Your electrician makes the connection.
