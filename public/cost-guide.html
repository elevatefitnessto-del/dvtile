What drives the cost of a luxury floor

  • Floor preparation. Large panels and stone need a flat, stiff subfloor. Levelling and membranes are often the difference between quotes.
  • Material and format. Book-matched slab, waterjet inlays and fragile stone take longer to plan, move and set than standard tile.
  • Layout. Herringbone, diagonal and vein-matched layouts mean more cuts and more waste than a straight lay.
  • Waterproofing. In showers, a bonded membrane and a flood test add cost and remove most of the risk.
  • Access and protection. Occupied homes, elevators and finished floors elsewhere in the house add protection and handling time.

How to compare quotes

  1. Ask for a line-item quote that separates preparation, membranes, installation, trims and sealing.
  2. Ask for a layout drawing showing where joints, cuts and veins fall.
  3. Ask which waterproofing system will be used and whether the shower is flood-tested before tile.
  4. Ask for proof of WSIB coverage and liability insurance, and the length of the workmanship warranty, in writing.
  5. In Ontario, a written estimate cannot be exceeded by more than 10 percent without your consent.
