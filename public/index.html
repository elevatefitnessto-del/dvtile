What we install

We are installers first. One crew lays out, prepares and sets every surface in your home, from the entry floor to the shower ceiling, and stays on the job until the last joint is sealed.

  • Marble and natural stone floors

    Calacatta, Carrara, Nero Marquina, travertine, limestone and slate, in tile or full slab. Book-matched, vein-matched and laid to a plan you approve before we cut.

  • Large-format porcelain and slabs

    Panels up to 160 × 320 cm for floors, walls and fireplaces. Levelling systems and full mortar coverage keep lippage under a millimetre across the room.

  • Showers and wet rooms

    Curbless entries, linear drains, benches, niches and steam rooms. Every shower is waterproofed and flood-tested before tile goes on.

  • Kitchen backsplashes and feature walls

    Full-height slab, zellige, mosaic and 3D tile, with outlets, window returns and range hoods scribed in rather than worked around.

  • Heated floors

    Electric in-floor heating under stone and porcelain, installed and tested with the thermostat before the floor is set.

  • Terraces, pools and outdoor stone

    Thermal limestone, flamed granite and 2 cm outdoor porcelain on terraces, steps and pool surrounds, built to handle freeze and thaw.

  • Stairs, fireplaces and details

    Solid stone treads, mitred risers, fireplace surrounds, thresholds and metal trims finished by hand.

  • Restoration and polishing

    Honing, polishing, crack repair and sealing for marble and stone floors that have lost their finish.

How a project runs

Stone is unforgiving. Most of the work that makes a floor look effortless happens before the first piece is set. For typical Toronto prices, see the cost guide.

  1. Site assessment

    We measure, check the subfloor and walls for level and moisture, and talk through the rooms, the materials and your schedule.

  2. Layout and quote

    You get a written quote and a layout drawing showing where every joint, cut and vein falls. Slabs are dry-laid for your approval before cutting.

  3. Preparation

    Levelling, uncoupling membranes, waterproofing and heating go in first, each one tested before it is covered.

  4. Installation

    The same crew sets every surface, with finished floors protected and the site cleaned at the end of each day.

  5. Sealing and handover

    Grout and stone are sealed, and you get a care guide for each material in your home.

Questions we hear

If yours isn’t here, add it to your site assessment request and we’ll answer it before we meet.

Do you supply the stone and tile, or only install it?

Both. As an installation partner of Olympia Tile + Stone and Centura, we can order anything in their ranges and coordinate delivery to site. If your designer has already sourced material elsewhere, we install that too.

Can you install marble or porcelain over in-floor heating?

Yes. We install electric heating mats under stone, porcelain and tile, test them with the thermostat before the floor goes down, and use an uncoupling membrane so the heat cycle does not crack joints.

Do you work with interior designers, architects and builders?

Yes. Send drawings and finish schedules and we price from them, attend site meetings, and work to the designer’s layout and detailing.

How do you keep large-format tile and slabs flat?

We check the floor for level first and correct it, then set each piece with full mortar coverage and a levelling system. The aim is lippage under a millimetre, which matters most with large panels and raking light from big windows.

Which areas do you cover?

Toronto and the GTA, including Forest Hill, Rosedale, Lawrence Park, the Bridle Path, Hoggs Hollow, Yorkville, the Kingsway, Vaughan, Kleinburg, King City, Richmond Hill, Markham, Oakville and Mississauga.
