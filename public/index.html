The collection

Natural stone, handmade tile and large-format porcelain, chosen for kitchens, baths, entries and the floors between them. Every surface here is shown as full pieces, not chips on a ring.

    Also in the showroom: large-format porcelain slabs, mosaics, and trim in brushed and unlacquered metals.

    One project in the room at a time.

    Choosing stone for a home is slow work. Veining that looks quiet in a sample can run loud across a floor; a glaze changes colour between morning and evening light. We book one appointment at a time so you can lay pieces side by side, take them to the window, and hold them against your cabinetry and paint.

    Interior designers and architects are welcome with their clients, or on their own with drawings in hand.

    1. Book a time

      Tell us about the rooms and your timeline. We confirm an appointment by email.

    2. Bring the project

      Plans, measurements, a cabinet door, a paint chip. We pull materials before you arrive.

    3. Leave with samples and a quote

      Take pieces home to see in your own light. Quantities and lead times follow in writing.
