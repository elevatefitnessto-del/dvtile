Kitchen backsplashes and feature walls

Book a site assessment

What we install

  • Full-height slab backsplashes in natural stone or porcelain, book-matched where the stone allows
  • Handmade zellige and glazed tile
  • Mosaics, subway and herringbone patterns
  • 3D and fluted decorative tile
  • Stone ledges and shelves with space for hidden LED strips

Details that make a backsplash look built in

  1. We set out the layout from the focal point, usually the range or sink, so cuts land evenly at the ends.
  2. Outlets and switches are cut cleanly in the tile or slab, with box extenders where needed.
  3. Window returns and the underside of hoods are finished in the same material, not left with exposed edges.
  4. Edges meet cabinets and counters with a consistent joint or a metal trim you choose in advance.

Questions

Can the backsplash match my stone countertop?

Yes. If your countertop fabricator supplies slab offcuts or extra slab, we can install a matching full-height backsplash.

How long does a backsplash take?

It depends on size and material. We give you a schedule in writing with the quote after the site assessment.
