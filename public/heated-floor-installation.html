Heated floors under stone and tile

Electric in-floor heating under marble, natural stone and porcelain, installed and tested before the floor is set.

Book a site assessment

What we install

  • Electric heating mats and cables for bathrooms, kitchens, entries and mudrooms
  • Uncoupling and heating membranes that combine both layers
  • Thermostats and floor sensors placed for your electrician to connect
  • Heated shower floors and benches where the system allows

How we install it

  1. We lay out heating to cover the walkable floor and avoid areas under cabinets and tubs.
  2. Resistance is tested when the mat arrives, after it is laid, and after it is covered, so a damaged wire is caught before the stone goes on.
  3. A licensed electrician makes the final connection to the thermostat.
  4. The stone or tile is set over the system with full coverage, then the heat is brought up gradually once the mortar has cured.

Questions

Is heated flooring safe under marble?

Yes, when it is installed with an uncoupling layer and the heat is introduced slowly after curing. Stone holds and spreads heat well.

Can heating be added to an existing floor?

Not under a floor that stays. Heating goes in during a new floor installation, so it is best planned with the renovation.
